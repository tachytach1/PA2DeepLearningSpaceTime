Model A uses standard convolutional recurrent layers without explicit reflection or rotation equivariance. Because of this, I predict that it will exhibit nonzero consistency errors under both horizontal mirroring and 90° rotation. I also expect some degradation in transformed forecasting accuracy, although it cannot be guessed beforehand which transformation will produce the most error as training has not been run. 

The optical-flow analysis of the first 50 training videos retained 103,050 valid pixels and measured a median motion speed of 3.318 pixels per frame. At 2× and 3× sampling speeds, this corresponds to approximate displacements of 6.64 and 9.95 pixels between observations. Model A's two 3×3 recurrent convolutions provide an estimated local spatial reach of approximately 8 pixels in our original image.

I therefore predict that forecasting accuracy will deteriorate noticeably at 2 times and that Model A will struggle substantially at 3 times, where the estimated displacement exceeds its local propagation scale.
